Settings and usage notes

Rule mode uses a generic example vocabulary, not a personal profile. Searches default to US jobs posted within 48 hours, newest first. Verify dates for manually opened or pasted jobs. Role families focus on data science, statistics, experimentation, and marketing analytics. Keyword coverage is not a hiring probability; missing keywords do not prove missing skills.

Salary midpoints are references only. Review multiple ranges and unclear units. The extension does not fill salary history or submit applications. Records are stored in this browser.

Supply your own resume and preferences in API mode. Rule mode suggests keywords; optional API mode sends your supplied text after confirmation and does not rewrite your resume.
